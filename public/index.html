Frequently Answered Queries

Academic & Access Policies

Why does a Google Drive folder ask for permissions?

↓

Access to official club folders is governed directly by Google Drive permissions configured by the folder owner and department administrators. If you see an access request screen, please log in with your institutional student email account (or email the club coordinator to request viewing access).

What is the difference between Autonomous and VTU Scheme folders?

↓

AMC Engineering College operates as an Autonomous Institution for recent batches, featuring specialized internal curricula and exam structures. Earlier batches or specific programs adhere to the Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) curriculum. Choose your respective scheme using the selector above.

How can students contribute verified notes and previous question papers?

↓

The NEURON NET Academic Club regularly collects handwritten toppers' notes, faculty revision materials, and exam question papers. Please contact your student class representative or reach out directly to the NEURON NET club coordinators.

Is VTU Circle affiliated with AMC Engineering College?

↓

No. VTU Circle is an independent third-party educational reference portal providing publicly available VTU syllabus copies, question papers, and GPA calculators. It is provided here strictly for convenience and is not endorsed by or affiliated with AMC Engineering College.
